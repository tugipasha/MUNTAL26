Model United Nations Conference

Conference · Chapter I

Built on diplomacy, research and debate.

MUNTAL — Model United Nations at Tınaztepe Anatolian High School — brings together secondary-school delegates to practise the procedures and responsibilities of the United Nations. The work is academic, deliberate and held to a high standard of preparation.

In İzmir, student organisers host an international conference defined by substantive committees, clear procedure and an atmosphere appropriate to multilateral diplomacy. Schools, advisors and delegates are welcome to apply.

Committees · Chapter II

Six committees. Six agendas.

  • 01 UNSC — Security Council Peace & security · Preventing escalation in emerging geopolitical conflicts
  • 02 DISEC — Disarmament & International Security Global security · Regulation of autonomous weapons systems
  • 03 UNHRC — Human Rights Council Human rights · Protection of civilians during armed conflicts
  • 04 UNEP — Environment Programme Environment · Climate resilience and adaptation strategies
  • 05 ECOSOC — Economic and Social Council Development · Reducing inequality through sustainable development
  • 06 WHO — World Health Organization Public health · Pandemic preparedness and international cooperation

Letters · Chapter III

A welcome to our delegates.

On behalf of the Secretariat, it is my honour to invite you to MUNTAL 2027. This conference was founded to provide a disciplined, internationally minded setting in which young diplomats learn not only to speak, but to listen, negotiate and draft policy with care.

You will be expected to arrive prepared. Read your background guides, refine your position papers, and enter committee ready to defend your nation's interests while seeking solutions others can accept.

We look forward to welcoming you to İzmir this March.

— The Secretariat, MUNTAL 2027

Apply · Chapter IV

Apply to MUNTAL 2027

Join delegates, chairs and student leaders from across the region for three days of diplomacy, negotiation and global dialogue.

MUNTAL 2027 accepts a limited number of participants across each committee and support role. Applications are reviewed by the Secretariat in order of receipt and academic preparedness. Selection is based on experience, motivation and suitability for assigned responsibilities.

Submit your materials before the final deadline. Incomplete applications will not be considered for committee placement.
